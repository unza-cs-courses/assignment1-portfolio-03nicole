About Me

I am a web development student who enjoys turning ideas into clean, responsive interfaces that are easy to navigate. My background includes coursework in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, along with hands-on projects where I focused on layout, typography, and accessibility. I am especially interested in how thoughtful design choices make technology feel welcoming and intuitive. When I build a page, I start with structure and semantics, then layer in styles that support clarity and hierarchy. I also test across breakpoints to make sure the experience is consistent on phones, tablets, and desktops. My goal is to grow into a front-end developer who can collaborate across teams and deliver experiences that are both beautiful and reliable. What makes me unique is my balance of detail-oriented visual design with a practical, problem-solving mindset, so every feature looks good and works well.
